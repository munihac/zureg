# Venue

    MuniHac 2026 will take place at the TNG Technology Consulting offices,
    Beta-Straße 13a, 85774 Unterföhring. The venue can be reached by S-Bahn
    and Bus.

    Google Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/place/?q=TNG+Technology+Consulting+GmbH&ftid=0x479e74c74cfca785:0xb774bd7390f72580
    Open Street Map: https://www.openstreetmap.org/?mlat=48.186007&mlon=11.654246#map=19/48.186007/11.654246

    Please use the side entrance to the building (Beta-Straße 13a).

# Schedule

    https://munihac.de/2026.html#schedule

    MuniHac will start on Friday, October 9th at 9:00, and end around 17:00
    on Sunday, October 11th.

    You can find the preliminary schedule on the MuniHac website. There might be
    some updates in the next weeks, so be sure to check again before the event!

    You can check in at the registration desk on Friday between 8:30–11:30, or
    Saturday between 9:00–10:30. If you plan to arrive outside these time
    windows for some reason, please let us know in advance (via email or Slack).

# Keynotes, talks and workshops

    While the main focus of MuniHac is in getting together and hacking on

      Haskell
    * And more talks!

    The full list can be found on https://munihac.de/2026.html#programme, we'll
    update the page regularly.
